[center][b]Funnel Titan[/b][/center]

[b]Antonoff-Titan K225 "Charybdis"[/b]

This specialized excavation variant modifies the K225 hull by removing the default excavator and the two forward docking bays. In their place is a large intake funnel protected by a simple metal mesh, allowing rocks to feed directly into internal grinders. To make physical room for the funnel and the internal grinder machinery, thrusters have been relocated outwards.

[u][b]Additions:[/b][/u]
[indent]* [b]Funnel Grinders[/b]: Toggled alongside the main excavator. Crushes incoming ringroids.[/indent]
[indent]* [b]Grinder Upgrades[/b]: Options for 1, 3, or 5 grinder pairs.[/indent]
